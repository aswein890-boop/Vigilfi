# FAQ — VigilFi

Q: Is my data stored?

A: No. VigilFi performs all calculations locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted or stored by the application.

Q: How is the score calculated?

A: The score is a weighted index combining emergency coverage, debt pressure, savings health, income stability, city cost risk, and dependents. See `docs/scoring.md` for exact formulas.

Q: Can I use this for tax or investment planning?

A: No. VigilFi is a planning and education tool. It does not replace professional financial, tax, or legal advice.
